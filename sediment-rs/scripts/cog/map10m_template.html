How this map was made

River channels were mapped at 10 m from how often each pixel was open water in __FREQN__ low-cloud Sentinel-2 views, searching within 400 m of streams draining at least 25 km². Lakes, reservoirs, tanks, lagoons and the coastal strip are excluded.

Sediment is the red-band suspended particulate matter of Nechad et al. (2010) on Sen2Cor surface reflectance, averaged as a geometric mean over clear views (median __VIEWS__ per pixel). A pixel counts when it is cloud-free, at least 50 m from cloud, and clearly water (NDWI above 0.15).

Values are uncalibrated estimates: compare rivers, reaches and seasons rather than reading single numbers. Channels narrower than about 20 m, or hidden under trees, are not mapped. A few coastal canals and lagoon creeks pass the river test too, such as the Dutch Canal near Puttalam and creeks on the Jaffna peninsula. Seasons: NE monsoon Dec–Feb, inter-monsoon 1 Mar–Apr, SW monsoon May–Sep, inter-monsoon 2 Oct–Nov.
